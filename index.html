How to make a deal

Before you message us, have ready

  • The other person's Telegram username
  • The price you agreed, in ₦
  • What the account has (level, skins, what it's linked to)
  • Whether you're buying or selling

  1. Agree on the price and the account with the other person.

  2. Message @Officialgamevault_MM. We'll confirm the deal with both of you.

  3. Buyer pays us, only to the details we give you on Telegram.

  4. We check the payment, then tell the seller to go ahead.

  5. Seller gives the buyer the account.

  6. Buyer checks it (under 30 minutes) and confirms.

  7. We pay the seller. Done.

Rules

  • Buyers: check the account before you confirm.
  • Sellers: wait until we say the payment is secured.
  • Only trust @Officialgamevault_MM.

If you're buying

Your money stays with us until you have the account and you're happy it's right. The seller never gets paid first.

If you're selling

You hand over the account only after we confirm the payment is real. No fake receipts and no chasing buyers.

If something goes wrong

Buyer finds a problem
Tell us inside the check window and send proof, like screenshots or a screen recording.
We freeze the money
Our team looks at both sides and decides within 1 to 3 hours. The money is either released to the seller or returned to the buyer.
Buyer says nothing
If the buyer doesn't confirm or report a problem in time, the money is released to the seller.
